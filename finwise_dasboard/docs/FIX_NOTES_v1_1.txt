FINWISE 1.1 — CONFIRMED-BILLS BUGFIX
====================================

ISSUE
  A manually confirmed bill saved successfully but did not reduce Safe to
  Spend. If no prior monthly history was present, the month-end forecast
  ignored that confirmed bill as well.

FIX
  1. Upcoming confirmed bills now automatically count as future essential
     reserves, reducing Safe to Spend without another manual entry.
  2. Upcoming confirmed bills now affect the month-end forecast even when
     the current pace-only estimator is used (no prior spending history).
  3. Multiple bills in the same category are added together.
  4. A manual essential reserve with the same category as future bills is
     merged by taking the larger amount to avoid obvious double-counts.
  5. Confirmed future bills are reserved against available cash as well.
     'Other unpaid cash obligations' should exclude confirmed bills.
  6. UI and user documentation have been updated to clarify these rules.

REPRODUCIBLE EXAMPLE
  On November 3, 2026, assuming no historical data or other constraints:
    Monthly budget                        2500.00
    Posted expense                         112.00
    Confirmed bill November 20             600.00
    Safe to Spend BEFORE bill             2388.00
    Safe to Spend AFTER bill              1788.00
    Month-end forecast BEFORE bill        1120.00
    Month-end forecast AFTER bill         1720.00

BOUNDARIES
  - Bills only count as upcoming when due after the report as-of date and
    before or on the last day of that month.
  - If spendable cash or another constraint is already tighter, Safe to
    Spend might still appear unchanged despite a new bill.
  - If a bill is paid early, manually remove or update it so it is not
    counted a second time before the due date. No bill-payment matching yet.
  - Expense forecasts remain simplified heuristic estimates.
